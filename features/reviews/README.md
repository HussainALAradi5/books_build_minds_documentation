# Reviews

A purchaser may leave one active review with a rating and comment. Only the author may edit it; the author or an administrator may soft delete it. Deleted reviews stay hidden in the public list. Administrators can turn on **Show deleted reviews** to see active and deleted reviews together; deleted entries are marked and cannot be edited or deleted again.
